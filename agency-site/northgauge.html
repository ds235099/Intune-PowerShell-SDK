Offer 02 · Northgauge

Northgauge

Workflow automation and a dashboard the owner can read.

Northgauge runs the handoffs you have approved, then shows four readings from your own records. Confirmed revenue is the sum of amounts you mark as confirmed. It is not a prediction.

Discuss Northgauge Open the separate calculator
Owner dashboardYour records only

Enquiries

How many new enquiries arrived in the period you select.

Response time

How long passed from an enquiry to the first reply.

Bookings

How many jobs were marked booked.

Confirmed revenue

The total of amounts you have confirmed. A record, not a forecast.

Workflows

Automation you sign off first.

A rule names who is notified, which stage changes, and when a reminder is allowed. Anything you do not want sent automatically stays with a person.

  1. 01

    Stage change

    Move a qualified enquiry to the next stage you defined.

  2. 02

    Late reply

    Notify the owner when a first reply misses the response-time rule you set.

  3. 03

    Open loop

    Remind you about a quote or a booking that is still open.

  4. 04

    Your call

    Stop or edit a workflow without treating the dashboard as a target.

Limits

What the dashboard is not.

  • Showing confirmed revenue does not create that revenue.
  • This page does not display sample customers or sample totals.
  • The ROI calculator on the home page is a different tool. It uses the visitor's assumptions. It is not a Northgauge report.
