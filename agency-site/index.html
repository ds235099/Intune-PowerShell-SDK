ROI calculator

Arithmetic from your assumptions.

This is not the Northgauge dashboard and it is not a forecast. Move the controls. The result is only the arithmetic of the numbers you enter.

A 20% lift on a 15% conversion rate is 18% in this model. Enquiry volume and job value stay constant. Fulfilment cost, tax, churn, and unpaid work are not included. A support plan on this site starts from A$299 a month. The cost control is your own assumption, not that price.

Plain answers

What this site will and will not do.

Does the site take payment?

No. The prices are indicative, exclusive of GST and third-party fees. A proposal is agreed in writing, away from this page.

Will the calculator tell me what I will earn?

No. It repeats the assumptions you set. It is not a promise of income, and it is not Northgauge.

Where does an enquiry go?

Into a draft in your own email app, and only after a contact address is set in config.js. That address is blank until the owner sets it. The website does not store the form.
