Offer 01 · Clearline

Clearline

Enquiry capture, qualification, and follow-up until a job is booked.

Clearline is the follow-up system. An enquiry from your website, or from another source you name, is captured, checked against the questions you set, and kept moving until the job is booked or you close it.

  1. 01

    Capture

    New enquiries land in one queue with the details you ask for.

  2. 02

    Qualify

    Your questions separate work you can take from work you should decline.

  3. 03

    Follow up

    Reminders go out on the schedule you approve. A person can stop a message.

  4. 04

    Booked

    The path ends when the job is booked, or when you mark it closed.

Starting scope

Enquiry and Booking Setup.

  • Owner notification and follow-ups

Limits

What Clearline will not claim.

  • It does not promise a number of booked jobs.
  • It does not publish sample customers, sample revenue, or a countdown.
  • It emails people who have enquired, on rules you approve. It is not a cold-outreach tool.
  • The home-page calculator is a separate illustration. It is not a Clearline result.

The other offer

Need the owner view as well?

Northgauge is the workflow automation and the dashboard: enquiries, response time, bookings, and confirmed revenue. It is a separate offer. It is added to a Clearline build only when the written scope includes it.
